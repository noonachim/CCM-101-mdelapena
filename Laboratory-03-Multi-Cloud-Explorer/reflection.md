# Reflection

Completing Mission 3 helped me understand how AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform differ and how each platform can be selected based on specific business requirements. The cloud platform that impressed me the most was AWS because it provides a large variety of services that can support different types of applications and organizations. I also learned that the most popular cloud provider is not always the best choice for every organization.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, security, monitoring, and other cloud services. They also provide global infrastructure that allows organizations to deploy applications and resources in different locations. Although their service names and features may differ, many services provide similar functions.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure because it integrates with the Microsoft ecosystem. An AI research company may benefit from Google Cloud because of its AI and machine learning capabilities. A startup may focus more on scalability, available services, and managing its resources efficiently.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud platform requires more than knowing technical features. I need to understand the client's goals, budget, existing technology, expected growth, and application requirements before making a recommendation.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added research documents, comparison tables, recommendations, screenshots, and a reflection. Organizing the laboratory work in Markdown helped me practice technical documentation and made my portfolio more structured and professional. Overall, this mission improved my understanding of multi-cloud environments and helped me think more like a Cloud Solutions Consultant.
